# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- **Harassment** — Unwelcome conduct, including offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, disability, or other personal characteristics
- **Abuse** — Threatening, intimidating, or hostile behavior
- **Hate speech** — Promoting hatred against any group or individual
- **Scams** — Attempting to defraud or deceive community members
- **Impersonation** — Pretending to be someone else
- **Spam** — Repeated, unsolicited messages or promotional content
- **Malicious behavior** — Attempting to harm the project, its users, or its infrastructure
- **Exploitation** — Taking advantage of others for personal gain
- **Sexual attention** — Unwelcome sexualized comments or advances
- **Doxxing** — Publishing others' private information without permission
- **Trolling** — Deliberately inflammatory or disruptive behavior

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub issues, PRs, and discussions
- Project documentation
- Community forums
- Social media presence
- Public events and meetings

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All project maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
