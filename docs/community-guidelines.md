# Community Guidelines

Welcome to Weekend. These guidelines cover what is and is not allowed on the
platform. Following them helps keep Weekend a safe and enjoyable space for
everyone.

---

## 1. Be respectful

- Treat others with kindness. Harassment, bullying, or hate speech of any kind
  will not be tolerated.
- Do not send repeated unwanted messages.
- Respect people's boundaries. If someone says no or asks you to stop, stop.

## 2. Be yourself

- Use your real first name. Nicknames that obscure identity are not allowed.
- Be honest about your age, gender, and relationship intent.
- Do not impersonate anyone, including public figures.

## 3. Keep it appropriate

### Photos

- Upload only your own photos.
- No explicit or suggestive images.
- No photos with minors (your children, siblings, etc.) unless they are clearly
  family photos and you are present.
- No screenshots of messages or other users' profiles.
- No logos, watermarks, or promotional graphics.

### Prompt answers and bio

- Be authentic and engaging.
- No hate speech, threats, or personal information (phone number, social media
  handles, employer details, etc.).
- No spam, scams, or promotional content.
- No links to external sites for solicitation.

## 4. No commercial activity

- Do not use Weekend for business promotion, advertising, or solicitation.
- No escorts, sugar dating, or transactional relationship services.
- Do not sell or trade accounts.

## 5. Respect safety features

- Do not try to circumvent blocks or reports.
- Do not create fake accounts to interact with someone who has blocked you.
- Do not share someone else's private information from the app (doxxing).

## 6. Meeting in person

- If you choose to meet someone from the app, meet in a public place.
- Tell a friend or family member where you are going.
- Be cautious of anyone who professes love quickly or asks for money.

## 7. Don't scam or defraud

- Romantic scams, phishing, and financial fraud are strictly prohibited.
- Do not ask for money, gifts, or investments from other users.
- Report anyone who asks you for money.

## 8. Report violations

If you see something that violates these guidelines:

1. Open the user's profile.
2. Tap the three-dot menu → **Report**.
3. Select the appropriate category and add details.

You can also use the **Safety Centre** to report from chat or from the discovery
deck.

## 9. Consequences

- First violation: warning or temporary restriction.
- Repeated or serious violations: account suspension or permanent ban.
- Underage users: immediate suspension pending age verification.

## 10. Updates

These guidelines may be updated. Continued use of the app after changes means you
accept the updated guidelines.

---

**Need help?** Use **Safety Centre → Contact Support** to reach a human reviewer.
