## 2. Full description

```
Weekend
Make Every Weekend Brighter.

Meet people nearby who actually want the same thing you do.

Weekend is a free dating and social discovery app for Android. Tell us what
you enjoy and who you want to meet, and Weekend shows you people nearby who
share it — no endless scrolling, no paywall on the first swipe.

HOW IT WORKS
Create a profile, set your date of birth, add your interests and turn on
location. Weekend then shows nearby profiles you can like or pass on. When
two people like each other, it is a match, and the chat opens.

WHAT IS FREE
Creating an account, building a profile, liking, passing, matching and
chatting are all free. Weekend does not charge to match or to message.

FEATURES
• Nearby discovery — see people around you, with a distance filter you control
• Filters that actually stick — gender, age range, distance, relationship
  intent and shared interests, applied on the server
• Real chat — send messages to your matches in real time
• Weekend Plans — create or join plans for things to do locally
• Photo verification — submit a selfie for verification and earn a badge
• Voice intros — record a short voice intro so people hear you first
• Safety Centre — block or report anyone, from anywhere in the app
• Passkeys and 2FA — sign in with a passkey or a second factor
• Biometric app lock — require Face ID or fingerprint to open Weekend
• Open source — the entire app is on GitHub under the MIT licence

PRIVACY AND SAFETY
Weekend is for adults 18 and over. Age is checked on the server, not just in
the app. Your exact coordinates are never shown to other people: distance is
calculated on our servers and shown as a rounded number, and only an
approximate location leaves your phone. Your photos are private until you post
them. Block and report are one tap away from every profile and every chat.

Ads in Weekend are contextual. They are not built from your profile and your
data is never sold.

Delete your account any time from Settings, or request deletion by email.

Weekend is free and open source.
```

## 7. App access (reviewer credentials)

Play requires reviewer credentials for any app that needs sign-in. Weekend
requires an account, so this is mandatory.

Create a **dedicated reviewer account**. Never give Google a real user's
private account.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Username | *e.g. `play-reviewer@weekend.app`* |
| Password | *generate a strong one, store it in the team password manager* |
| MFA | Leave **off** for the reviewer account |
| Passkey | Not required for the reviewer account |
| Navigation instructions | See below |

### Navigation instructions to paste into Play Console

```
1. Launch Weekend and tap "Create account".
2. Sign up with the credentials above. Email verification is not required.
3. Onboarding asks for date of birth, gender and interests. Weekend enforces
   an 18+ minimum; enter an adult date of birth.
4. Add at least 4 photos in Edit Profile (a profile cannot become
   discoverable below the 4-photo minimum).
5. Enable Location when prompted to see nearby discovery working. Denying
   location still allows browsing the Explore tab.
6. Discovery, Filters, Profile, Likes, Chat, Plans and Safety Centre are all
   reachable from the bottom navigation bar without extra setup.
7. Safety Centre (Settings > Safety Center) contains Block and Report.
   Settings > Delete Account starts account deletion.
```

Seed the reviewer account with at least 4 approved photos, or discovery will
appear empty and be judged as broken.
